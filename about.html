Behind the curtain

Founded in 2016 by eight friends and one borrowed harmonium. Today we are the campus's loudest, proudest creative family.

Vision

A campus where every student can discover and share their art without fear of the audience.

Mission

Train, produce and celebrate student performers through workshops, mentorship and fearless live shows.

What we do

Every week, something is happening on our stage.

Weekly workshops

Dance, vocals, acting and instrument labs led by seniors and guest artists.

Original productions

Two full-length plays and one musical each year.

Inter-college circuit

Competitive teams for fests across the state.
